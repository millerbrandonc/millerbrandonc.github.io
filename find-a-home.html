How leasing works

From tour to keys.

Two partners, one simple process. Here is who you will work with at each step.

  1. With our property manager

    Find a listing

    Our homes are listed by our property management partner on major rental sites.

  2. With our property manager

    Tour the home

    Book a tour through the listing. Tours are with our property manager or agent.

  3. With us

    Apply online

    After your tour, we email you a secure invitation to apply through Avail.

  4. With us

    Review

    We review your application and follow up with next steps.

  5. With us

    Sign your lease

    Review and sign your lease electronically, right in the portal.

  6. With us

    Move in

    Set up rent payments and autopay, and get your keys.

Renting safely

How to know a listing is ours

  • We never ask for payment by wire transfer, gift card, or cryptocurrency.
  • We never ask for money before you have toured the home.
  • Applications, leases, and rent payments go through our Avail resident portal only.
  • Not sure about a listing? Email hello@dnbinvestments.com with the link before you share personal information or send money. We will confirm whether it is ours.

Spotted a fake listing? Report it to the website and to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
