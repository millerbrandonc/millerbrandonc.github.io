Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

DNB Desert Investments, LLC (“DNB Desert Investments,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information we collect through our website, www.dnbinvestments.com (the “Site”).

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information, including information collected from visitors to our Site through our contact forms and other features. It also explains the privacy rights available to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (collectively, the “CCPA”).

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information we collect through our Site, including when you:

  • visit or browse the Site;

  • submit an inquiry or request through one of our forms, such as our contact, interest list, or property submission forms;

  • communicate with us by email, phone, or through the Site; or

  • interact with Site features that use cookies or analytics tools.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to information collected offline, through third-party websites or services not operated by us, or through third-party platforms that are linked from our Site.

Our Site links to third-party platforms used for resident-related services: Avail (through December 31, 2026) and Innago (beginning January 1, 2027). We do not collect resident application or portal information directly through our Site. If you choose to use one of these platforms, your information will be collected and processed by that platform in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information through our Site:

A. Identifiers

Examples include:

  • name;

  • email address;

  • phone number;

  • internet protocol (IP) address; and

  • other online identifiers associated with your browser or device.

B. Internet or Other Electronic Network Activity Information

Examples include:

  • browsing activity on our Site;

  • pages viewed;

  • referral sources;

  • approximate location based on IP address;

  • device and browser information; and

  • technical information recorded in standard server logs maintained by our website hosting provider.

C. Commercial or Inquiry Information

Examples include:

  • the contents of messages you send us;

  • inquiry details submitted through our forms; and

  • records of your communications with us.

D. Professional or Business Information

If you contact us regarding investment opportunities, partnerships, or business matters, we may collect professional or business-related details that you voluntarily provide.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through our Site, such as government identification numbers, financial account information, precise geolocation, health information, or similar sensitive data.

3. Sources of Personal Information

We collect personal information from the following categories of sources:

  • Directly from you, when you fill out a form on our Site, email us, call us, or otherwise communicate with us;

  • Automatically from your device or browser, through standard server logs maintained by our website hosting provider; and

  • From service providers acting on our behalf, such as website hosting and form storage providers.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for the following business and commercial purposes:

  • to respond to your inquiries and communicate with you;

  • to provide information about our business, properties, investment opportunities, or services;

  • to operate, maintain, and improve our Site;

  • to monitor Site performance, traffic, and visitor engagement;

  • to detect, prevent, and address security issues, fraud, or misuse;

  • to maintain business records and internal administration;

  • to comply with applicable legal obligations; and

  • to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We use personal information only as reasonably necessary and proportionate for these purposes.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our Site does not set cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Like most websites, our website hosting provider automatically records standard server log information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, the pages requested, and the date and time of each request, to operate and secure the Site.

When you submit a form on our Site, your submission is processed by Web3Forms, a form-processing service, which delivers it to our business email.

We do not currently use:

  • Google Analytics;
  • Meta Pixel;
  • advertising retargeting tools;
  • session replay tools; or
  • similar third-party advertising technologies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. How We Disclose Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients for business purposes:

  • Website hosting and form-processing providers, including our website host and Web3Forms;

  • Email and productivity service providers, including systems used to receive form submissions and maintain related records;

  • Cloud-based document or spreadsheet providers, including Google Workspace, where inquiry information may be stored for internal business use; and

  • Legal, compliance, or governmental entities, when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect our rights, property, or safety.

We do not knowingly disclose personal information to data brokers.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

In general, we use the following retention guidelines:

  • Contact form submissions and general inquiries: up to 3 years after our last substantive communication with you;

  • Business correspondence and inquiry-related records: up to 3 years, or longer if reasonably necessary for business recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or legal compliance;

  • Server log information: for the period retained by our website hosting provider, or as otherwise reasonably necessary for Site administration and performance review; and

  • Legal or compliance records: as long as required by applicable law, regulation, or legitimate business need.

We may retain information for a longer period when necessary to investigate issues, enforce our agreements, comply with legal obligations, or protect our rights.

8. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the following rights under the CCPA, subject to certain exceptions and limitations:

A. Right to Know

You may request that we disclose:

  • the categories of personal information we have collected about you;

  • the categories of sources from which the personal information was collected;

  • the business or commercial purposes for collecting, selling, or sharing personal information;

  • the categories of third parties to whom we disclose personal information; and

  • the specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.

B. Right to Delete

You may request that we delete personal information we collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions.

C. Right to Correct

You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.

D. Right to Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights under California law.

E. Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

F. Right to Limit Use and Disclosure of Sensitive Personal Information

We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would trigger a right to limit under California law.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 16 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information collected through our Site. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
